About the Project

What is Saving of Swedovia?

Saving of Swedovia (SOS) is a research game being developed to study ethical decision-making in crisis management. Participants take on professional roles — emergency responders, officials, coordinators — and face realistic scenarios where resources are limited, time is short, and every choice has consequences.

When professionals must balance competing values under pressure — public safety vs. individual rights, short-term response vs. long-term recovery — there is a risk of both moral stress and suboptimal outcomes. Yet we know surprisingly little about how these trade-offs actually play out, what drives different prioritization strategies, and how the public perceives the legitimacy of these decisions.

SOS aims to generate new knowledge about ethical decision-making during climate-related crises and how better tools and training can strengthen both individual and societal resilience.

Research Phases

Phase 1

Literature Review & Theoretical Framework

Systematic review of research on ethical dilemmas in crisis management. Development of the theoretical framework.

Phase 2
Current

Interviews & Game Development

Conducting in-depth interviews with practitioners in crisis management. Their real-world experiences directly inform the game's scenarios and decision architecture. Simultaneously designing and developing the simulation game.

Phase 3

Playtesting & Iteration

Testing with practitioners and stakeholders. Iterating on game mechanics, scenarios, and the ethical dilemma framework.

Phase 4

Research Studies

Conducting studies using the game to investigate how ethical dilemmas are handled in practice and what consequences different priorities have for crisis response.

Phase 5

Game Release & Open Data

Public release of the game, research data, and accompanying analysis as open resources for education, training, and further research.
