About the Project

What is Saving of Swedovia?

Saving of Swedovia is a research-based serious game currently in development at Linnaeus University. The game places players in the role of decision-makers who must navigate complex ethical dilemmas that arise during climate-related hazards and crises.

Drawing on real-world scenarios and practitioner experiences, the game aims to serve as both a pedagogical tool and a platform for understanding how ethical reasoning shapes decisions under pressure. By combining normative questions—what ought we do?—with empirical insights—what are we actually doing?—the project bridges the gap between theory and practice.

The project is generously funded by Vetenskapsrådet (the Swedish Research Council) and the Crafoord Foundation.

Research Phases

Phase 1

Literature Review & Framework

Systematic review of existing research on ethical dilemmas in crisis management and climate-related hazards. Development of the theoretical framework guiding the project.

Phase 2
Current Phase

Interviews & Game Development

We are currently conducting in-depth interviews with practitioners to understand the real ethical dilemmas they face during climate-related crises. These insights directly inform the game's scenarios and decision architecture. Simultaneously, the game is being designed and developed.

Phase 3

Testing & Iteration

Playtesting sessions with practitioners and stakeholders, iterating on game mechanics, scenarios, and the ethical dilemma framework based on feedback.

Phase 4

Analysis & Publication

Final data analysis, academic publications, and broader dissemination of findings. Release of the game as an open resource for education and training.

Get Involved

Participation

There are two ways you can contribute to the Saving of Swedovia project. Whether you want to stay informed or actively participate in shaping the game, we would love to hear from you.

📨

Stay Informed

Are you a practitioner working in crisis management, emergency services, or related fields? Sign up to receive updates about the game's development and be among the first to know when playtesting opportunities become available.

🎙

Participate in an Interview

We are seeking practitioners who would like to share their experiences and help shape the game through a research interview. Your insights into the ethical dilemmas you face in your work are invaluable to making the game realistic and meaningful.

Opportunities

Collaborate With Us

Research Collaboration

We welcome collaboration with researchers and practitioners who share our interest in ethical decision-making, crisis management, serious games, and pedagogical innovation. Whether you are interested in contributing to the current project or exploring new directions together—such as adapting the game framework for new contexts, conducting comparative studies, or developing complementary research—we are eager to connect.

Please reach out to Joel Martinsson to discuss potential collaborations.
