About the game

What is Saving of Swedovia?

Saving of Swedovia is an interactive research game that explores how people navigate ethical dilemmas during climate hazards. Players take on the roles of politicians, civil servants and first responders in the fictional country of Swedovia, facing difficult decisions where competing values clash and there are no clear right answers.

The game is open access: free for anyone to play, and free to use in teaching and training. The choices made by adult players who agree to take part are used as research data, helping us understand how people think ethical dilemmas in a crisis should be handled.

The project is generously funded by the Swedish Research Council and The Crafoord Foundation.

A serious game, made for research

Saving of Swedovia is a serious game: a game made for a purpose beyond entertainment. We use the answers from the game in our research on how people think crises should be handled. That is why it matters that you choose the way you think people should act, not what you think is expected of you. There are no right or wrong answers.

We do not collect any information that can be linked to you as an individual.

Inside the game

Along the way you meet the people handling the crisis, and make decisions together with them.

From real experiences to a game

Saving of Swedovia did not start as a game. It started with the people who make these decisions in real life, and grew step by step through research.

  1. Listening to practitioners

    We began by interviewing practitioners in crisis management about the hardest decisions they have faced, and the values that pulled in different directions.

  2. Turning stories into scenarios

    Their experiences became concrete dilemmas. We then tested the scenarios on a large scale: for each profession, a survey was sent to a representative sample of the Swedish population.

    Read more about the survey in Research →

  3. Building Swedovia

    Step by step, the scenarios were drawn, written and programmed into an interactive game set in the fictional country of Swedovia.

  4. Playtesting

    Practitioners and stakeholders played early versions. Their feedback shaped the scenarios, the characters and how decisions are made in the game.

  5. Released in 2027

    The game was released to the public: free for everyone to play, and free to use in teaching and training.

Education

How to use Saving of Swedovia for teaching and practice

  • Free to use
  • No account needed
  • Swedish and English

The game is a ready-made starting point for discussions about ethics, priorities and responsibility in crisis management. It can be used by practitioners – police, rescue services, healthcare, politicians and civil servants – in training and exercises, and by students and citizens in schools, university courses and public discussions about how society should handle crises. It is completely free: anyone may use it, without a licence, an account or any cost.

A typical session has three parts. Participants first play, individually or together as a group on one screen. Then the group talks through the choices: which values clashed, what was prioritised, and why. Finally, participants can compare their choices with how other players and professions chose, using the data explorer.

Survey

Alongside the game, we carried out large-scale surveys built on the game's scenarios, one for each profession: police, rescue services, healthcare, and politicians and civil servants. Each survey was sent to a representative sample of the Swedish population, about 4,000 people per survey.
